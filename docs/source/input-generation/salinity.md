Delta salinity boundary conditions used in CalSim 3 water quality modeling.

The 5 salinity variables represent constant or repeating boundary conditions for Delta salinity modeling. These variables do not require stochastic generation as they are held constant in CalSim 3 simulations or follow predetermined repeating patterns. This approach reflects the decision that Delta salinity modeling in CalSim 3 does not require the full complexity of stochastically-varying boundary conditions, given the dominant role of operational rules and flow management in determining actual salinity outcomes.
